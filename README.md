# Borozdov Doodle

A theme from the Borozdov collection. Two faces — light **Dotgrid**, a doodled planner on
dot-grid paper, and dark **Nightowl**, the same planner on inkwell navy after hours. Inkwell
navy does the work, coral marks links and the caret, mint badges carry ticks and tags, a
butter wash is the highlighter, and headlines are tracked open like hand lettering.

![Borozdov Doodle in light mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/doodle/main/screenshots/light.png)

![Borozdov Doodle in dark mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/doodle/main/screenshots/dark.png)

## Principles

- **A planner, not a spreadsheet.** The note sits on dot-grid paper whose dots travel with
  the page; the sidebar is warm stone and cards are white.
- **Inkwell navy is the workhorse.** Text, rules, the open file and the main button are
  navy, never pure black. Colour is punctuation: coral for links and the caret, mint for
  ticks and tags, butter for the highlighter.
- **Headlines in a hand-printed voice.** Arsenal Bold for the title and the two largest
  headings, tracked open the way lettering is; everything else in the platform's sans,
  compact and tight.
- **Soft, friendly shapes.** 8px corners on everything you press, 16px on cards with a
  warm stone shadow instead of a cold grey one, 24px on the feature card.
- **A feature card for the plain note.** Navy on paper by day, paper on navy by night.

## Features

- Light and dark modes, following Settings → Appearance → Base color scheme
- A dot-grid page in reading and editing views; embeds and hover previews stay plain cards
- Callouts as white cards with the title in the type's colour; the plain note is the
  feature card
- Mint checkboxes with a navy tick, and tags as mint badges
- Quiet editing: no focus ring around the note, its title or form fields while you type;
  property names read as labels, not boxed fields
- Text colours meet WCAG contrast on both faces
- The phone layout keeps the same colours and shapes
- No `!important`: every rule can be overridden with a CSS snippet

## Installation

**From the community directory:** Settings → Appearance → Themes → Manage, search for
**Borozdov Doodle**, then **Install and use**.

**By hand:** download `manifest.json` and `theme.css` from the
[latest release](https://github.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/doodle/releases/latest) into
`<vault>/.obsidian/themes/Borozdov Doodle/`, then choose Borozdov Doodle under
Settings → Appearance → Themes.

## Font

Arsenal Bold (© 2012 The Arsenal Project Authors) is embedded in `theme.css` as base64
WOFF2 under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 — see [`fonts/OFL.txt`](fonts/OFL.txt). One
weight, Latin and Cyrillic, for the title and the two largest headings only.
